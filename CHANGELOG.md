# Second Wind release notes

## Alpha 1.1 (October 21, 2026)

**Solo mode**
- A Companion switch in Settings: With Wren or Solo. Solo hides Wren, her chat, the bond and her voice. Voice cues, stats, tests and everything else stay
- New players pick one on first launch
- Switching back brings Wren back with everything she knew

**Wren's new face and moods**
- Redrawn face: softer jaw, real eye whites, lined lids, a fuller smile
- Moods that change with what's happening: happy after a session, proud when you hit your goal or keep a streak, calm during calming and sleep sessions, sleepy late at night, thinking while she writes back, and fired up or concerned in chat when the moment calls for it
- The mood shows on her portrait caption

**Wardrobe**
- 8 outfits: Hoodie, White tee, Team jersey, Denim jacket, Cozy knit, Varsity jacket (with your initial), Blazer and the Day Ones jacket
- New outfits unlock at Bond 3, 5, 7, 10, 15 and 20. "Match her mode" keeps each personality's own outfit

**She talks more**
- 24 new recorded lines: one at the start of a session, one at the halfway point, and one when you finish, in each of her 8 personalities
- Uses the same "Wren's voice" switch in Settings

**Weekly check-in**
- Every Sunday (and again Monday for last week), a card on Breathe: sessions, minutes, days practiced, bond XP and a day-by-day chart
- Wren writes a short note on your week and picks one focus tool for next week
- "Your week so far" lives on Wren's tab any day

## Alpha 1.0 (October 21, 2026)

The first official build. Everything below is new.

**Breathe**
- 19 breathing techniques across Calm, Focus, Recover, Sleep and Energy, including the Physiological Sigh, Box, 4‑7‑8, Bellows Breath, Cyclic Hyperventilation, NSDR and Trataka
- 5 Flows that chain techniques into one session: Brain Fog, Clutch Up, Post-Match Reset, Night Down and Morning Ignition
- Browse: the full library, with search and filters
- Breath tests (Full Hold, Slow Exhale, Empty Hold) and CO₂ table training, with personal bests
- Visual, audio, voice and haptic cues on every session

**Learn and reflect**
- Lab: the neuroscience behind each tool, with sources and how strong the evidence is
- 9 Solfeggio frequencies, with an honest look at what the science does and doesn't show
- Reflect: the traditions behind the practices, plus a journal
- Library: favorites and saved insights

**Progress**
- Profile with XP, levels, Breath Rating ranks, attributes, daily quests, weekly challenges and trophies

**Wren, your coach**
- Chat that picks a tool and starts it for you, in 8 personality modes, with a recorded voice
- A daily question, a daily horoscope, and a note from her each day
- Bond with Wren: 20 levels, "+XP" moments, unlocks, memory notes you control, and a "Your rhythm" section that learns your schedule
- 2.5D animation: she follows your finger, breathes with you during sessions and reacts in chat
- Customizable look: 10 presets, plus skin tone, hairstyle, hair color, eyes and highlight streak

**Under the hood**
- Works offline once installed to your home screen
- Backup and restore between devices
- An on-screen error banner and an error log, for alpha testing

## Known limits in Alpha 1.0
- On iPhone, audio pauses if you lock the screen mid-session. This is a web-app limit that a future App Store build would fix.
- In this version, history stays on the device it was made on. Use Settings → Back up to move it.
- Wren's live chat needs the optional server described in the README.
